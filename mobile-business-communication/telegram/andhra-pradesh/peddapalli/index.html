Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal?

Yes, as long as you follow Telegram's Terms of Service and your local marketing laws. We ensure all campaigns comply with applicable regulations.

Do recipients need to follow my channel first?

For channel broadcasts, yes — users must follow your channel. For direct messages via our bot integration, users must have initiated a conversation with your bot first, per Telegram's anti-spam policy.

What is the difference between a Telegram channel and a group?

A channel is a one-to-many broadcast tool (up to unlimited subscribers, only admins post). A group is a two-way conversation space (up to 200,000 members). Channels are better for announcements; groups are better for community engagement.

How fast is Telegram message delivery?

Telegram delivers messages with sub-second latency globally across all platforms (Android, iOS, web, desktop). There are no carrier dependencies or SMS routing delays — it is pure internet protocol delivery.

Can I send files, images, and videos via Telegram broadcasting?

Yes. Telegram supports files up to 2GB, including images, videos, PDFs, audio, polls, and interactive buttons. This makes it significantly more capable than SMS or even WhatsApp for rich content distribution.

What industries use Telegram broadcasting most?

Crypto and DeFi projects (community alerts), financial news services (market updates), digital media (breaking news), e-learning platforms (course updates), and political campaigns (constituency communication) are the heaviest Telegram broadcasting users.

Can my Telegram bot collect data or run surveys?

Yes. Our bot integration supports inline keyboards, polls, quiz modes, and form-like conversation flows that collect structured responses, which are then pushed to your CRM or database via webhook.

What is the cost per message compared to WhatsApp or SMS?

Telegram messaging costs 11p per message on our platform — comparable to SMS but with much richer capabilities (files, buttons, media). WhatsApp Business API conversations are billed per 24-hour session window (£0.05–£0.08 per conversation), making Telegram more predictable for high-frequency alerts.
