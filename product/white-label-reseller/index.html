Frequently Asked Questions

Branding

Will my clients see your name?
No. The platform is completely "White Label". Your clients only see your brand, your domain, and your support links.

Profit

How much can I make?
You buy credits at wholesale prices and sell them at retail prices. Most partners make a 50-200% margin on top of subscription fees.

Support

Who helps my clients?
You provide first-line support to your clients, and we provide back-end technical support to you.

Scaling

Can I have my own resellers?
Yes, our Elite plan allows you to create "Sub-Resellers" who sell under your brand.

Payments

How do I get paid?
The system integrates with Stripe. When your clients pay, the money goes directly into your bank account.

Contract

Is there a long-term contract?
No, our plans are month-to-month. You can cancel at any time.

Technical

Do I need a server?
No, we host everything on our high-speed, secure global infrastructure.

Updates

Do you add new features?
Yes, we are constantly updating the platform. New features appear in your dashboard automatically at no extra cost.
